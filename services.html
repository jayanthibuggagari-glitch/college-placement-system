Our Services

Smart placement solutions for students, companies and college placement administrators.

What We Provide

CareerConnect provides a centralized platform to manage college placement activities. It helps students find jobs, companies share opportunities and administrators manage the recruitment process.

🎓

Student Management

Store and manage student details such as name, roll number, branch, CGPA, email and phone number.

🏢

Company Management

Maintain company details, locations, websites and recruitment information.

💼

Job Management

Display job openings, job descriptions, salary packages and eligibility requirements.

📝

Online Applications

Help students apply for available jobs through the placement management system.

📊

Application Tracking

Track application progress through statuses like Applied, Shortlisted and Selected.

🔐

Admin Management

Provide administrators with a centralized way to manage placement-related information.

How CareerConnect Works

01. Register

Students create their account and provide their details.

02. Explore Jobs

Students view available jobs and check eligibility.

03. Apply

Eligible students submit their job applications.

04. Track Status

Students check their application progress.
